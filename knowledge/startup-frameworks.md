# Startup Frameworks & Methodologies

## Lean Startup Methodology

### Core Principles
1. **Build-Measure-Learn**
   - Build MVP quickly
   - Measure user response
   - Learn and iterate

2. **Validated Learning**
   - Test assumptions with real users
   - Use data to make decisions
   - Pivot when necessary

3. **Innovation Accounting**
   - Track meaningful metrics
   - Focus on actionable data
   - Measure progress accurately

### Implementation Steps
1. Identify problem
2. Develop hypothesis
3. Build MVP
4. Measure results
5. Learn and pivot or persevere

## Business Model Canvas

### 9 Building Blocks

1. **Value Propositions**
   - What value do you deliver?
   - Which problems are you solving?
   - What needs are you satisfying?

2. **Customer Segments**
   - Who are your customers?
   - What are their personas?
   - How do you reach them?

3. **Channels**
   - How do you deliver value?
   - Distribution channels
   - Communication channels

4. **Customer Relationships**
   - How do you acquire customers?
   - How do you retain them?
   - Relationship types

5. **Revenue Streams**
   - How do you make money?
   - Pricing models
   - Revenue sources

6. **Key Resources**
   - What resources do you need?
   - Physical, intellectual, human, financial

7. **Key Activities**
   - What must you do?
   - Production, problem-solving, platform/network

8. **Key Partnerships**
   - Who are your partners?
   - Suppliers, alliances
   - Joint ventures

9. **Cost Structure**
   - What are your costs?
   - Fixed vs variable costs
   - Economies of scale

## MVP Development Framework

### MVP Types

1. **Concierge MVP**
   - Manual service delivery
   - Test demand before building
   - Learn customer needs deeply

2. **Wizard of Oz MVP**
   - Looks automated, manual behind scenes
   - Test user experience
   - Validate before building backend

3. **Landing Page MVP**
   - Single page explaining product
   - Collect email signups
   - Measure interest

4. **Single Feature MVP**
   - Build one core feature
   - Test value proposition
   - Expand based on feedback

5. **Prototype MVP**
   - Interactive mockup
   - User testing
   - Validate UX before development

### MVP Prioritization

**MoSCoW Method:**
- **Must have:** Core functionality
- **Should have:** Important but not critical
- **Could have:** Nice to have if time permits
- **Won't have:** Out of scope for MVP

## Product-Market Fit Framework

### Indicators of Product-Market Fit
- 40%+ of users would be very disappointed without your product
- Users are actively recommending your product
- Word-of-mouth is driving growth
- Retention rates are high
- Customer acquisition cost is decreasing

### How to Achieve Product-Market Fit
1. Talk to customers constantly
2. Measure the right metrics
3. Iterate quickly based on feedback
4. Focus on one customer segment initially
5. Solve a real, painful problem

- **Sprint Review:** Demo completed work

## Common Startup Mistakes

1. **Building without validation**
   - Assuming you know what customers want
   - Not talking to users early
   - Ignoring feedback

2. **Scaling too early**
   - Premature hiring
   - Over-investing in infrastructure
   - Expanding before product-market fit

3. **Running out of cash**
   - Poor financial planning
   - Underestimating costs
   - Not fundraising early enough

4. **Wrong team**
   - Co-founder conflicts
   - Skill gaps
   - Misaligned vision

5. **Ignoring competition**
   - Underestimating incumbents
   - Not differentiating
   - Missing market shifts
