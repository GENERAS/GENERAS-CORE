## Delivery Models

### Fixed-Price Projects
- Clear scope defined upfront
- Fixed budget
- Defined timeline
- Best for well-defined projects

### Hourly Consulting
- Flexible engagement
- Pay for actual hours
- Ongoing support
- Best for advisory and consultation

### Retainer Agreements
- Monthly commitment
- Priority support
- Reserved hours
- Best for long-term partnerships

### MVP Development
- Quick prototype
- Core features only
- Fast to market
- Best for startups and validation
